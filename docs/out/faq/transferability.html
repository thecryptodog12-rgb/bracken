LOXLEY Transferability

LOXLEY Transferability

🚫

This page describes upstream Interfold's FOLD token, not Loxley's. Loxley is a fork of theinterfold/interfold (opens in a new tab); no LOXLEY token has been issued, no auction has been held, and none of the fundraising, valuations, allocations or unlock schedules below belong to this project. The text survives here as inherited documentation and must be rewritten or removed before Loxley makes any claim of its own.

⚠️

Loxley is not deployed yet. Contract addresses on this page are placeholders. Loxley is a fork of theinterfold/interfold (opens in a new tab) targeting Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663); the addresses that used to sit here belonged to the upstream Interfold deployment on Ethereum and would have sent you to someone else's contracts. They are archived in packages/loxley-contracts/upstream-interfold-deployments.reference.json.

General LOXLEY transferability is scheduled as part of the Loxley launch sequence on Aug 19.

This guide explains when transferability can be enabled, what changes once it is active, how auction participants can claim LOXLEY, and where to find official Loxley resources.

⚠️

Use only official Loxley links and verified contract addresses. Do not trust unsolicited DMs, copied contract addresses, impersonator accounts, or unofficial claiming and trading links.

When does general LOXLEY transferability begin?

General LOXLEY transferability can be enabled at or after:

Aug 19 at 14:00 UTC (10 AM ET)

Unix timestamp: 1787148000

At or after that timestamp, anyone can call the tge() function on the LOXLEY contract to enable general transferability.

The timestamp does not automatically enable transfers by itself. General transferability begins once the tge() transaction has successfully executed onchain.

The function is permissionless, meaning it can be called by anyone once the timestamp has passed. It does not depend on a specific Loxley-controlled account.

Official Loxley channels will confirm once general transferability has been enabled.

What changes once general transferability is enabled?

Once general LOXLEY transferability is enabled:

  • LOXLEY can be transferred between addresses without the launch-period general transfer restriction.
  • Holders can interact with LOXLEY through supported onchain interfaces.
  • LOXLEY can be used for supported network functions, including ciphernode bonding where available.
  • Allocation-specific vesting, lockups, or contractual restrictions continue to apply where relevant.

General LOXLEY transferability does not override restrictions that apply to a specific LOXLEY allocation.

Is LOXLEY transferability the same as Network Alpha?

No. General LOXLEY transferability and Network Alpha are distinct milestones within the broader Loxley launch sequence.

General LOXLEY transferability changes the transfer state of LOXLEY.

Network Alpha is the first mainnet operating phase of the Loxley network.

For more on LOXLEY and its role in the network, see:

  • LOXLEY Token
  • LOXLEY Tokenomics

I participated in the LOXLEY auction. When can I claim?

Claiming for LOXLEY Auction 2 is expected to open immediately after the auction closes on Aug 19 at 13:00 UTC (9 AM ET).

Allocated LOXLEY and any unspent USDC can then be claimed through the verified auction interface.

Claiming and general LOXLEY transferability are separate actions. Auction 2 closes one hour before general LOXLEY transferability can be enabled.

Start from the official LOXLEY Auction page (opens in a new tab).

For current auction and claiming information, see the LOXLEY Auction FAQ.

Do I need to do anything to LOXLEY I already hold?

General LOXLEY transferability does not itself require holders to claim through the Auction 2 interface.

Any vesting, lockup, or contractual restrictions applicable to a specific allocation continue to be governed by the terms of that allocation.

For currently published allocation and unlock information, see LOXLEY Tokenomics.

Can LOXLEY be used for ciphernode bonding?

LOXLEY supports ciphernode bonding as part of the economic operation of the Loxley network.

Ciphernode operators bond LOXLEY to become eligible for network duties and economically accountable for correct participation.

For current operator requirements, setup instructions, and bonding information, see:

  • Ciphernode Operators
  • Running a Ciphernode
  • Registration & Bonding

Current operator documentation is the source of truth for network participation requirements and bonding mechanics.

Does general transferability remove vesting or lockups?

No. General transferability does not override vesting schedules, contractual restrictions, or other conditions that apply to specific LOXLEY allocations.

See LOXLEY Tokenomics for currently published allocation and unlock information.

What is the verified LOXLEY contract?

The canonical LOXLEY token contract on Ethereum Mainnet is:

0xTBD_NOT_DEPLOYED

View the LOXLEY contract on Etherscan (opens in a new tab)

The tge() function that enables general LOXLEY transferability is called on this contract.

Always verify the LOXLEY token contract through an official Loxley source before interacting with a market, wallet, or third-party interface.

How do I stay safe around LOXLEY transferability?

Periods around token transferability can attract impersonators and fraudulent links.

Before interacting with LOXLEY:

  • Start from an official Loxley website, documentation page, or verified social account.
  • Verify the LOXLEY contract address before signing a transaction.
  • Do not trust contract addresses, claiming links, or trading links sent through unsolicited DMs.
  • Do not send assets to anyone offering manual claiming, migration, unlocking, or support.
  • Never share your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
  • Review the connected network, destination, and transaction details before signing.

Loxley team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet backup, or access to your wallet.
