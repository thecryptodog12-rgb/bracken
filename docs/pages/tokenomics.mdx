## Community Allocations

### Foundation Treasury

Loxley has been built in the open and is freely available as open-source software under the
LGPLv3 license. A portion of supply is allocated to Loxley Foundation. Grants may be awarded
proactively to support priority work or retroactively to recognize meaningful contributions.

### Unsold CCA Tokens

Tokens that were allocated to the Uniswap CCA that were not sold are to be returned back to the
foundation for further fundraising and/or liquidity support.

### Airdrop

This allocation is reserved for eligible community participants and ecosystem contributors.
Distribution criteria, eligibility requirements, and timing will be announced separately.

## Other

### Gnosis Guild

This allocation recognises Gnosis Guild's early role and is subject to a 48-month release schedule.

### Investors

Loxley, previously developed under the name Enclave, has completed the following fundraising
rounds.

- Pre-seed | $20m valuation
- Seed | $25.8m valuation
- A Legion round | $25.8m valuation
- Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction | ETH 14.4k valuation (~$25.8m at time of close)

All investor allocations will become transferable at the TGE date.

### Team and Advisors

Allocated to the core team and advisors who supported Loxley's early development.
