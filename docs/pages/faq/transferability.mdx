# LOX Transferability

General LOX transferability is scheduled as part of the Loxley launch sequence on Aug 19.

This guide explains when transferability can be enabled, what changes once it is active, how auction
participants can claim LOX, and where to find official Loxley resources.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Use only official Loxley links and verified contract addresses. Do not trust unsolicited DMs,
  copied contract addresses, impersonator accounts, or unofficial claiming and trading links.
</Callout>

## When does general LOX transferability begin?

General LOX transferability can be enabled at or after:

**Aug 19 at 14:00 UTC (10 AM ET)**

**Unix timestamp:** `1787148000`

At or after that timestamp, anyone can call the `tge()` function on the LOX contract to enable
general transferability.

The timestamp does not automatically enable transfers by itself. General transferability begins once
the `tge()` transaction has successfully executed onchain.

The function is permissionless, meaning it can be called by anyone once the timestamp has passed. It
does not depend on a specific Loxley-controlled account.

Official Loxley channels will confirm once general transferability has been enabled.

## What changes once general transferability is enabled?

Once general LOX transferability is enabled:

- LOX can be transferred between addresses without the launch-period general transfer restriction.
- Holders can interact with LOX through supported onchain interfaces.
- LOX can be used for supported network functions, including ciphernode bonding where available.
- Allocation-specific vesting, lockups, or contractual restrictions continue to apply where
  relevant.

General LOX transferability does not override restrictions that apply to a specific LOX
allocation.

## Is LOX transferability the same as Network Alpha?

No. General LOX transferability and Network Alpha are distinct milestones within the broader
Loxley launch sequence.

**General LOX transferability** changes the transfer state of LOX.

**Network Alpha** is the first mainnet operating phase of the Loxley network.

For more on LOX and its role in the network, see:

- [LOX Token](/faq/fold-token)
- [LOX Tokenomics](/tokenomics)

## I participated in the LOX auction. When can I claim?

Claiming for LOX Auction 2 is expected to open immediately after the auction closes on Aug 19 at
**13:00 UTC (9 AM ET)**.

Allocated LOX and any unspent USDC can then be claimed through the verified auction interface.

Claiming and general LOX transferability are separate actions. Auction 2 closes one hour before
general LOX transferability can be enabled.

Start from the official [LOX Auction page](https://www.theloxley.com/fold-auction).

For current auction and claiming information, see the [LOX Auction FAQ](/faq/auction).

## Do I need to do anything to LOX I already hold?

General LOX transferability does not itself require holders to claim through the Auction 2
interface.

Any vesting, lockup, or contractual restrictions applicable to a specific allocation continue to be
governed by the terms of that allocation.

For currently published allocation and unlock information, see [LOX Tokenomics](/tokenomics).

## Can LOX be used for ciphernode bonding?

LOX supports ciphernode bonding as part of the economic operation of the Loxley network.

Ciphernode operators bond LOX to become eligible for network duties and economically accountable
for correct participation.

For current operator requirements, setup instructions, and bonding information, see:

- [Ciphernode Operators](/ciphernode-operators)
- [Running a Ciphernode](/ciphernode-operators/running)
- [Registration & Bonding](/ciphernode-operators/registration)

Current operator documentation is the source of truth for network participation requirements and
bonding mechanics.

## Does general transferability remove vesting or lockups?

No. General transferability does not override vesting schedules, contractual restrictions, or other
conditions that apply to specific LOX allocations.

See [LOX Tokenomics](/tokenomics) for currently published allocation and unlock information.

## How do I stay safe around LOX transferability?

Periods around token transferability can attract impersonators and fraudulent links.

Before interacting with LOX:

- Start from an official Loxley website, documentation page, or verified social account.
- Verify the LOX contract address before signing a transaction.
- Do not trust contract addresses, claiming links, or trading links sent through unsolicited DMs.
- Do not send assets to anyone offering manual claiming, migration, unlocking, or support.
- Never share your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
- Review the connected network, destination, and transaction details before signing.

Loxley team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet
backup, or access to your wallet.
