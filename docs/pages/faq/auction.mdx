## What is LOXLEY Auction 2?

LOXLEY Auction 2 is the second public LOXLEY auction, run using Uniswap’s Continuous Clearing
Auction mechanism.

It follows the initial LOXLEY Auction held July 8–10, 2026.

Auction 2 is intentionally limited to **2% of total LOXLEY supply** and is being held around the
transition to general LOXLEY transferability to broaden distribution and help bootstrap deeper,
longer-term onchain liquidity.

## Why is there a second LOXLEY Auction?

Auction 2 is designed as a limited distribution step ahead of general LOXLEY transferability on
Aug 19.

It is intentionally small at **2% of total LOXLEY supply** and begins with a floor matching the
clearing price of the first LOXLEY Auction.

The auction provides a transparent, public mechanism for additional distribution before LOXLEY
becomes generally transferable, while supporting long-term onchain liquidity.

**50% of proceeds will be dedicated to long-term onchain liquidity support.**

Auction 2 closes shortly before general LOXLEY transferability can be enabled.

## What is a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction?

A Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) is a price-discovery mechanism for token distribution built on
Uniswap infrastructure.

- The auction runs across multiple discrete periods.
- Participants submit bids through the official Uniswap CCA interface.
- Successful bids within a period clear at the same clearing price.
- The minimum price for each subsequent period is the greater of the original floor price or the
  previous period’s clearing price.
- The minimum price may rise in later periods, but does not fall below the applicable prior clearing
  price or original floor.
- Any tokens not distributed earlier remain available in the final period.

By distributing tokens progressively and applying a uniform price within each period, the CCA is
designed to reduce gas competition, mitigate sniping, and allow clearing prices to develop through
auction participation.

[Read more about Uniswap CCAs](https://cca.uniswap.org).

Claiming and general LOXLEY transferability are separate actions.

## When does general LOXLEY transferability begin?

LOXLEY Auction 2 closes Aug 19 at **13:06 UTC (9:06 AM ET)**.

General LOXLEY transferability can be enabled shortly afterward, at or after **14:00 UTC (10 AM
ET)**.

At or after Unix timestamp **`1787148000`**, anyone can call the `tge()` function on the LOXLEY
contract to enable general transferability.

Once that transaction is executed, LOXLEY becomes generally transferable.

LOXLEY Auction 2, claiming, general LOXLEY transferability, and Network Alpha are distinct
milestones within the broader Loxley launch sequence.

## Does the auction determine the price of LOXLEY after transferability?

No. The auction determines allocations and a clearing price within the auction itself. It does not
determine LOXLEY’s market price once general transferability begins.

Future market prices cannot be predicted.
